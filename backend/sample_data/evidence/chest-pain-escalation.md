section: Acute chest pain
---

Chest pain with arm radiation, diaphoresis, dyspnea, syncope, or hemodynamic instability
should trigger immediate escalation. Early actions typically include vital signs, ECG,
cardiac biomarkers, and emergency evaluation rather than outpatient follow-up.

Clinical decision support should focus on severity assessment and rapid disposition rather
than diagnosis alone. When high-risk features are present, delays for non-urgent workup can
increase harm.
