section: Pulmonary nodules
---

Solid pulmonary nodules that increase in size over serial imaging should be treated as
higher-risk findings than stable nodules. Suspicious morphology, including spiculation,
increases concern for malignancy and supports escalation beyond routine surveillance.

For an enlarging pulmonary nodule above 8 mm, the next step generally includes advanced
imaging such as PET-CT and referral for tissue diagnosis or thoracic consultation when the
overall clinical picture supports it. The exact follow-up interval should also incorporate
baseline cancer risk, prior imaging stability, and patient comorbidities.
