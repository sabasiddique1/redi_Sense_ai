Clinical decision support outputs should be phrased as supportive recommendations, not
autonomous diagnoses or orders. Responses should stay conservative, avoid fabrication, and
encourage confirmation with the patient record, direct evaluation, and licensed clinical
judgment.

When patient context is incomplete, the safest recommendation is to surface the uncertainty,
outline the key red flags, and recommend appropriate escalation or evidence review.
